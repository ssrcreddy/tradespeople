How it works

Tradespeople Quotes & Invoices Agent: the AI drafts quotes from messy customer messages, the app does the pricing, and a person approves everything that gets sent.

Who is involved

There are four kinds of actor. The owner is the only one who decides anything, the AI only prepares drafts, and customers never log in.

  • Owner: can do everything, and is the only one who can change business settings, change the intake email address, export the data or delete the business.
  • Team member: the role exists in the code, but the app has no way to invite anyone yet, so today it's a single-owner app.
  • Customer: sends enquiries and receives quotes and invoices by email. They never use the app.
  • AI model: reads messages and suggests lines and questions. It never sets a price and never sends anything.

How to use it, as the owner

  1. Set up once: sign up, then enter your business and tax details and your rates. Your rates are what every quote is priced from.
  2. Add an enquiry: paste the message into a new job. Once email is set up, forwarded enquiries arrive by themselves.
  3. Review the draft: the AI splits the message into lines and the app prices them from your price book. Fix anything amber or unpriced, and answer or ignore the AI's questions.
  4. Approve and send: nothing goes to a customer until you do this.
  5. Record the customer's answer: customers reply by email or phone, so you mark the quote accepted or declined yourself. There's no customer login or online accept button.
  6. Invoice and record payment: an invoice can only be made from an accepted quote. You record payments yourself, because the app doesn't take card payments. The app sends reminders for you, once real email is set up.
